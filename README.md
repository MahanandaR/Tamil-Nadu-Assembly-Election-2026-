# Tamil Nadu Election Analytics Dashboard (2021 vs 2026)

## ◆ Project Overview
This project analyzes the Tamil Nadu Assembly Election 2026 using publicly available Election Commission of India (ECI) constituency-level data. The objective was to transform raw election results into compelling, fact-based stories through interactive Power BI visualizations while maintaining complete political neutrality.

The dashboard was developed as part of the AtliQ Media Election Analytics Challenge, where the focus was on storytelling, insight generation, and data visualization rather than political interpretation.

## ◆ Problem Statement
AtliQ Media wanted to create a data-driven election results program that focuses on facts rather than debates and opinions. Using only official ECI election data, the goal was to identify the most interesting stories from the 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly election and present them through clear, engaging, and easy-to-understand visualizations.

The analysis compares the 2021 and 2026 elections across all 234 constituencies to uncover seat shifts, regional dominance changes, electoral competitiveness, and voting patterns.

The project required the development of an interactive Power BI dashboard and supporting visual stories that answer key questions such as:

• How did party seat shares change between 2021 and 2026?

• Which constituencies changed winning parties?

• How did political dominance shift across different regions?

• How competitive was the 2026 election compared to 2021?

• Which constituencies recorded the closest and largest victory margins?

The final solution focuses on data storytelling, visual analytics, and objective reporting while maintaining complete political neutrality. 

## ◆ Objective
To build a comprehensive election analytics dashboard that converts raw election results into meaningful, fact-based stories through interactive visualizations, enabling viewers to understand electoral trends, regional shifts, seat flips, and margin patterns across all 234 Tamil Nadu constituencies.

#  Selected Research Stories

## ✦ 1. Overview Story
TVK wins 108 seats on debut — 163 of 234 constituencies change hands

▶ Key insights:

• Total Constituencies: 234

• TVK emerged as the largest party with 108 seats.

• 163 constituencies changed their winning party compared to 2021.

• Average voter turnout reached 86.1%.

• Tiruppattur recorded the narrowest victory margin of just 1 vote.

▶ Visuals:

• Party seat comparison (2021 vs 2026)

• Seat share distribution

• Electoral competitiveness breakdown

• Key election highlights

• Battle for Every Vote Analysis

## ✦ 2. Geographic Story

TVK swept Chennai Metro — AIADMK held Central, DMK only in Delta

▶ Key insights:

•  Chennai Metro became TVK's strongest region.

•  TVK secured dominance across 4 of 6 editorial regions.

•  AIADMK retained leadership in Central Tamil Nadu.

•  DMK maintained dominance only in Delta.

▶ Visuals:

•  Region-wise seat distribution

•  Regional swing analysis

•  Dominance shift indicators

•  Regional performance comparison

## ✦ 3. Flip Analysis Story

163 seats flipped — the largest political churn in Tamil Nadu's modern history

▶ Key insights:

• 163 constituencies changed winning parties.

• TVK absorbed significant seat share from both DMK and AIADMK.

• DMK lost 74 seats compared to 2021.

• South Tamil Nadu witnessed the highest number of constituency flips.

▶ Visuals:

• Sankey seat-flow diagram

• Party-to-party flip analysis

• Seat retention metrics

• Flip-rate calculations

## ✦ 4. Margin Story

Narrower victories, tougher battles

▶ Key insights:

• Average winning margin declined significantly compared to 2021.

• Photo-finish constituencies (margin below 5,000 votes) increased sharply.

• Landslide victories reduced considerably.

• Tiruppattur became the closest contest with a margin of just 1 vote.

▶ Visuals:

• Margin distribution comparison (2021 vs 2026)

• Closest winning constituencies

• Average margin analysis

• Electoral competitiveness categories
